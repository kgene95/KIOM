# Fig. 3(B) manuscript text candidate

## Methods

To re-evaluate the IKKβ docking model, the historical Xenopus structure was replaced with human IKKβ chain B from PDB 4KIK. The crystallographic KSA pose was redocked first using AutoDock Vina 1.2.7 and the fixed 30 Å cubic grid (exhaustiveness 32); the symmetry-aware heavy-atom RMSD was 0.3808 Å. Neutral vitexin-4″-O-glucoside (PubChem CID 56776173) was then docked using three independent seeds (20261004–20261006) under the validated protocol. Naringenin (CID 439246) was retained as a comparator. Pose reproducibility, steric clashes, pocket occupancy, and PLIP 3.0.1 interaction fingerprints were evaluated for the top pose from each seed.

## Results

The three neutral vitexin poses were highly reproducible (direct-coordinate heavy-atom RMSD 0.0485–0.1088 Å) and had Vina scores of −10.442 to −10.458 kcal/mol. All three poses passed the recorded clash and pocket-occupancy checks and produced 12–13 hydrogen bonds and six hydrophobic interactions in PLIP. The naringenin comparator produced scores of −9.289 to −9.329 kcal/mol and pose RMSD values of 0.0201–0.0461 Å. Thus, within this fixed neutral-state protocol, vitexin showed a more favorable calculated score than naringenin.

## Interpretation boundary

These results support a reproducible docking hypothesis for the human IKKβ pocket and justify replacing the historical 3RZF-based panel. They do not establish experimental binding, inhibition, target engagement, or pathway regulation. The primary result is conditional on the neutral vitexin state; charged-state sensitivity and independent ligand-strain quantification remain limited.
