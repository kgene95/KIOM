# Cross-section and evidence audit

Check alignment across:
- Title and Abstract
- Introduction objectives/hypotheses
- Methods and experimental design
- Results and figure/table order
- Discussion interpretation
- Conclusion
- Figure legends and supplements

Audit for:
- objectives with no corresponding results;
- major results not anticipated or contextualized in the Introduction when context is needed;
- methods that cannot generate the claimed endpoint;
- Discussion claims stronger than Results;
- abstract claims stronger than main text;
- mechanism claims based on surrogate or single-marker evidence;
- inconsistent group names, time points, doses, concentrations, units, sample sizes, statistical tests, and figure references;
- factual claims whose citations do not support the specific population/species, model, intervention, assay, endpoint, direction, or causal strength;
- selective omission of material contradictory or null findings when supplied.

When a central claim changes, perform a change-impact scan across Abstract, Introduction, Results wording, Discussion, Conclusion, figures/legends, highlights, and graphical-abstract text when present.
